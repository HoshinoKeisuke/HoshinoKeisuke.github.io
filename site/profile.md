I am a researcher in the [Computer Science Group](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~cs/) at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/), Kyoto University. I study category theory, especially (virtual) double categories and weak ω-categories.

[Curriculum vitae (PDF)](https://hoshinokeisuke.github.io/CV/keisuke-hoshino.pdf)

## Research interests

I am interested in formal category theory, (virtual) double categories, triple categories, and multiple categories. I also study weak ω-categories in the sense of Batanin and Leinster, and Grothendieck’s homotopy hypothesis.

My interests in computer science include realizability, homotopy type theory, and algebraic effects and their handlers.
